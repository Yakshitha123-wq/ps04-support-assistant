# Support Articles - NetConnect (fictional broadband/mobile provider)

## BILLING

### Article B1: Billing discrepancy - first occurrence
If a customer reports being charged more than their plan amount and this is their
first such complaint (no prior billing tickets on record in the last 90 days),
explain the most recent bill amount and plan amount from the account record.
If the amounts genuinely differ and there's no clear reason (e.g. a one-time
installation fee or pro-rated charge), offer to raise a billing review.

### Article B2: Payment not reflecting
If a customer says they paid their bill but it still shows as unpaid, ask for
the payment date and payment method (bank transfer, card, UPI, etc.) if not
already provided. Payments can take up to 48 hours to reflect. If more than
48 hours have passed since the stated payment date, this should be escalated
with the payment details for manual reconciliation.

### Article B3: Requesting a refund
Refunds for double-billing or overcharges are processed within 5-7 business
days once confirmed. If the account record shows a confirmed duplicate charge,
inform the customer of the timeline. If the duplicate charge is not clearly
visible in the account record, do not confirm a refund - this needs to go to
a human to verify the billing system directly.

## CONNECTION (WIFI/BROADBAND)

### Article C1: No internet connectivity
For a complete loss of internet connection, first ask the customer to confirm
whether the router lights are on and whether they've tried restarting the
router. If they haven't tried restarting, ask them to do so first. If they
have already restarted and it's still not working, this should be escalated
as a possible line or equipment fault.

### Article C2: Slow internet speed
If a customer reports slow speeds, check the account record for their
subscribed plan speed. Ask whether the slowness happens on all devices or
just one, and whether it's at specific times of day. If it's a specific
device issue, suggest checking that device's network settings. If it's
network-wide and persistent, escalate for a line quality check.

### Article C3: Installation delayed
If a customer's broadband installation is delayed, check the account record
for the scheduled installation date. If the current date is past the
scheduled date, apologize, confirm the new expected date is being escalated
to the field team, and log a follow-up. Do not guess a new date - only the
field team can confirm it, so this should be escalated with the original
scheduled date and how many days overdue it is.

## MOBILE NETWORK

### Article M1: No mobile network signal
If a customer reports no signal or "no service" on their mobile line, ask
whether this happens in one location or everywhere, and whether other
people on the same network have signal in that location. If it's
location-specific, this is likely a coverage/tower issue and should be
escalated with the location provided. If it's everywhere, ask them to
toggle airplane mode on and off first; if that doesn't help, escalate.

### Article M2: Call drops or poor call quality
If a customer reports dropped calls or poor call quality, ask whether it
happens on both cellular calls and any app-based (VoLTE/wifi calling) calls,
and whether it's a specific location. Suggest checking if wifi calling is
enabled if they're in a low-signal area. If the issue persists across
locations and call types, escalate as a possible network-side issue.

### Article M3: SIM not activating / new SIM issues
If a customer says a new SIM card isn't activating, confirm the activation
was requested more than 24 hours ago (activations can take up to 24 hours).
If it has been less than 24 hours, inform them of the timeline. If more than
24 hours have passed with no activation, this should be escalated with the
SIM number and activation request date.

## PLAN

### Article P1: Upgrading a plan
Customers can upgrade their plan at any time. Confirm their current plan from
the account record, then explain that an upgrade takes effect from the next
billing cycle and the new rate will apply from that date. No approval needed
for upgrades - this can be confirmed directly to the customer.

### Article P2: Data limit exceeded (mobile plans)
If a customer on a mobile plan reports their data has run out before the
billing cycle ends, check the account record for their plan's data limit.
If they've genuinely exceeded it, explain the limit and offer a data top-up
add-on. If the account record shows they should still have data remaining
according to the plan, this is a discrepancy that should be escalated.

### Article P3: Plan renewal and downgrade
Plan renewals happen automatically at the same tier unless the customer
requests otherwise. Downgrade requests take effect at the next renewal date,
not immediately. If a customer wants an immediate downgrade or a refund for
the difference, this falls outside standard policy and should be escalated.
